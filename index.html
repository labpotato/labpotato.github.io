Abstract

Background. Wet-lab research is full of small jobs that eat whole afternoons. Methods. Lab Potato is a set of free browser tools for wet-lab research. Each tool is one web page that runs in your browser. Results. Count colonies, label gels and microscopy figures, design Gibson primers, quantify protein, map freezer boxes, read papers, edit plasmids and keep a lab notebook. Nothing to install, no account, and your data never leaves your computer. Conclusions. No account. No trial period. No feature that disappears behind a plan.

Keywords: colony counting; gel labelling; Gibson assembly; microscopy figures; protein quantification; freezer inventory; lab notebook; plasmid editor; labpotato

Methods: what reaches your notebook, and how

Every analysis tool has a ↗ Lab Entry button. Press it and the result waits in Lab Entry until you file it under an experiment and a day. It all stays in this browser.

Lab EntryWaiting room → experiment → day
  • Text, a table and pictures arrive together, with the tool's name and version.
  • Live: an open Lab Entry tab shows a new result the moment you press the button.
  • Not connected: Cryobox Labeler and Doc Reader keep their own files.
  1. Press ↗ Lab Entry in the tool's header when the result looks right.
  2. It waits in the waiting room: kept in this browser's storage (not uploaded anywhere).
  3. File it: in Lab Entry, drag it, or pick an experiment and a day; it becomes part of that day's entry and report.
  4. Another computer? Use Save to file in the tool's Lab Entry box and open the file in Lab Entry there.
Gibson → Gel Labeler. Gibson passes its expected PCR sizes to the Gel Labeler's expected lane, and the ladder you use most comes first in both.
One address per tool. tools/lab-entry/ and its siblings always open the newest version, so bookmarks never go stale.

Declarations

About Lab Potato

Free tools for everyday lab work: counting colonies, labelling gels and microscopy images, planning Gibson assemblies, protein quantification, freezer boxes, a lab notebook and a paper reader.

Each tool is one web page that runs in your browser. Nothing to install, no account, and your data stays on your own computer.

Research use

For research use only. Not for clinical or diagnostic use. Check results before you rely on them.

Data availability

There are no data to share: nothing you open in a tool is uploaded anywhere.

Code availability

The source of every tool is public at github.com/labpotato/labpotato.github.io.

Funding

None. Powered by caffeine and an approaching thesis deadline.
