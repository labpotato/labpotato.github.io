FELICX Oligo Designer

Pick the base you want to read out. The tool places the one-base overlap between the flap and invading primers at that position, builds the deliberate 3′ mismatch on the invader, and works out the released flap, the fickle base and the ds-convertor variant, then shows you the whole cleavage junction as an annotated duplex. Everything runs in your browser; nothing is uploaded.

Paste the amplicon you intend to probe (the region your HDA primers copy), 5′→3′, DNA. The flap and invading primers are built antiparallel to this strand.

Click the base you want to interrogate — the junction / fickle base. The one-base flap–invader overlap is placed here; for SNP genotyping, click the polymorphic base.

Built on Aggarwal et al., FELICX, Biosens. Bioelectron. 222 (2023) 115002. Constant oligos are quoted from the paper’s Supplementary Table S1. Tm values are nearest-neighbour estimates, not a substitute for ordering-house calculators or a wet-bench screen. — Lab Potato
