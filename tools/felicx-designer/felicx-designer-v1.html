FELICX Oligo Designer

Paste an amplicon and get the four target specific oligos, each one printed base by base in its real position on the sequence: the flap primer with its constant arm lifting off, the invading primer with its deliberate 3′ mismatch, and the single base where the two overlap. Runs entirely in your browser.

Target amplicon

Paste the region your HDA primers will copy, 5′→3′. The flap and invading primers are built antiparallel to this strand. Around 70 to 120 base pairs suits thermostable HDA.

Junction base Placed automatically at the centre of the amplicon. Nudge it onto a polymorphic base to genotype a SNP, or to move the primer footprints off the HDA primer sites.
Method after Aggarwal et al., FELICX, Biosensors and Bioelectronics 222 (2023) 115002. Constant oligos quoted from that paper’s Supplementary Table S1. Tm values are nearest-neighbour estimates and do not replace an ordering-house calculator or a bench screen.
