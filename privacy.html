Privacy policy

Lab Potato Doc Reader (the Chrome extension and the Doc Reader web app) · Last updated 29 October 2026

Lab Potato is a set of free lab tools. Doc Reader has no Lab Potato server and no Lab Potato account: your highlights, notes and documents are kept on your own computer and, only if you turn it on, in your own Google account. Lab Potato never receives them.

Google user data

When you choose "Sign in with Google", Doc Reader asks for one permission: See, create, and delete its own configuration data in your Google Drive (scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata). With it, Doc Reader can read and write only the file it creates in Drive's hidden app-data folder, which holds your Doc Reader highlights, notes and documents so they appear on your other computers. It cannot see, open or change any other file in your Drive. The only other thing it reads is the email address of the signed-in account, which is shown in the library so you know which account is syncing; it reads nothing else in your Google account, such as contacts, mail or other files.

This data goes directly between your browser and Google. It is not sent to Lab Potato or to anyone else, it is not sold, it is not used for advertising, and no person reads it. Lab Potato Doc Reader's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

AI features (optional)

Doc Reader's AI editor works only if you add your own API key for an AI service you choose (for example Anthropic Claude, OpenAI or Google Gemini). When you ask it to edit a passage, the passage, its surrounding text and the document you are working on are sent from your browser directly to that service under your own account and its privacy terms. Nothing is sent to an AI service unless you ask for an edit, and data from Google Drive is never sent to one except as part of a document you choose to edit.

Website visit counts

The Lab Potato website counts page visits with GoatCounter, which records the page, browser type, screen size, country and referring site, without cookies and without identifying you. The extension itself does not count or track anything.

Removing your data

  • Delete highlights and notes inside Doc Reader, or remove the extension to delete its local storage.
  • Press "Sign out" in the extension's library to stop Drive sync and revoke Doc Reader's access. You can also remove access at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and delete the hidden app data from Google Drive (Settings → Manage apps → Lab Potato Doc Reader → Delete hidden app data).
  • Delete any backup files you saved.
