CFU/mL formula: count ÷ (dilution fraction × volume plated in mL), per FDA Bacteriological Analytical Manual, Ch. 3. Countable range (25–250 CFU/plate) per the same source is flagged, not blocked — some workflows count outside this range intentionally.
Background suppression: illumination is removed by a morphological top-hat transform, subtracting a grayscale opening computed with a structuring element larger than the largest colony (Sternberg, 1983), implemented with the constant-time running min/max of van Herk (1992). This replaces the flat-field background reference used in v2, which assumed even illumination across the selection.
Thresholding: automatic level by Otsu (1979), applied as a dual (hysteresis) threshold after Canny (1986): strong colony cores seed the mask and faint peripheries are grown from them, while isolated faint pixels are rejected. The low threshold is held above a median-plus-4-MAD noise estimate of the response map.
Touching colonies: exact Euclidean distance transform (Felzenszwalb & Huttenlocher, 2012); markers are selected by topological persistence (h-maxima criterion, Soille, Morphological Image Analysis, 2nd edn, 2004, §6.3) so that plateau ties inside one round colony cannot seed a false split; separation by marker-controlled watershed (Vincent & Soille, 1991).
Image adjustment: the local-contrast option is contrast-limited adaptive histogram equalisation (Pizer et al., 1987), 8×8 tiles with bilinear interpolation between tile mappings. Adjustments are applied to the image that is both displayed and measured. Note that heavy adjustment changes what the detector sees: for a defensible count, record the settings used, or leave them at default, since the top-hat stage already corrects uneven illumination.
Learning from examples: clicking example colonies estimates appearance class, median colony radius, area range and response threshold from those colonies directly. This is parameter estimation from user examples, not a trained classifier, and it is [INFERENCE] — a procedure assembled here rather than a published method.
Validation: the pipeline was checked against ten synthetic plates with known colony counts (vignetted white-on-dark with faint colonies, illumination-gradient dark-on-pale, chromogenic, an artefact plate carrying a scratch and a marker stroke, and resolution series at 900/1600/2400 px). Counts were exact or within one colony on all but the deliberately fused-triple plates. Two limitations are inherent: colonies overlapping by more than roughly half their radius cannot be resolved by any distance-transform method, and a colony physically touching a scratch merges with it. No manufacturer kit or third-party protocol is encoded here, so no community-modification toggle applies. The tool has not yet been benchmarked against a manually counted reference plate.
Validated on: synthetic touching-colony test images (two- and three-colony fused blobs; isolated colonies; sub-pixel debris) — see build notes. Not yet benchmarked against a manually-counted reference plate; treat automatic counts as a first pass and confirm with manual add/remove before reporting.
⚠ Biosafety reminder: this tool only processes a photograph of a plate. It provides no containment. Handle and image live culture plates under your institution's standard biosafety practice (appropriate BSL containment, PPE, and disinfection of surfaces/imaging equipment) before and after use.
