What reaches your notebook, and how

Every analysis tool has a ↗ Lab Entry button. Press it and the result waits in Lab Entry until you file it under an experiment and a day. It all stays in this browser.

Gel LabelerThe labelled gel figure, the lane list, band sizes and quantified bands↗ Lab Entry Colony CounterPlate pictures with the colonies marked, counts, CFU/mL and transformation efficiency↗ Lab Entry Protein QuantConcentrations and yield per protein, the standard curve, as a table↗ Lab Entry Microscopy LabelerThe figure panels and line-profile plots↗ Lab Entry Gibson AssemblyPrimers, the assembly map and the virtual gel↗ Lab Entry
Lab EntryWaiting room → experiment → day
  • Text, a table and pictures arrive together, with the tool's name and version.
  • Live: an open Lab Entry tab shows a new result the moment you press the button.
  • Not connected: Cryobox Labeler and Doc Reader keep their own files.
  1. Press ↗ Lab Entry in the tool's header when the result looks right.
  2. It waits in the waiting room: kept in this browser's storage (not uploaded anywhere).
  3. File it: in Lab Entry, drag it, or pick an experiment and a day; it becomes part of that day's entry and report.
  4. Another computer? Use Save to file in the tool's Lab Entry box and open the file in Lab Entry there.
Gibson → Gel Labeler. Gibson passes its expected PCR sizes to the Gel Labeler's expected lane, and the ladder you use most comes first in both.
